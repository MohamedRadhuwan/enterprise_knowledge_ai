COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Casual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 6 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Leave Approval:
Employees must submit a leave request through the employee portal.
Annual leave requests should normally be submitted at least 3 working days before the requested leave date.

Emergency Leave:
In emergency situations, employees should notify their manager as soon as possible.

Unused Leave:
Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the following year according to company policy.
